# The one blocklist that actually matters

The bounced addresses are housekeeping. **This** is the list that would have stopped Stanley Lo being cold-pitched from Jonathan's own address three weeks before Cherry emailed him about a live offer.

**I cannot build it.** It needs company data I have no access to — DocuSign, the deal mailboxes, whatever CRM or spreadsheet holds past transactions. Only someone inside can pull it.

---

## What goes in it

Every email address the company has ever done business with:

- **Past sellers and buyers** — anyone who signed anything.
- **The agents on the other side** of every deal. Stanley Lo was a counterparty's broker, not a client, and that was enough.
- **Anyone mid-deal right now.** Most urgent. A live counterparty getting cold acquisition mail is the worst version of this.
- **Title, escrow, lenders, inspectors** — anyone we work with repeatedly.
- **Everyone at those firms**, not just the one person. If Green Banker is off-limits, `maxlo@greenbanker.com` is too. That is why Max Lo got 16 emails.

---

## Where to pull it from

1. **DocuSign** — export all completed envelopes. Every signer's address. This is the best single source; it is a record of everyone who signed something.
2. **The deal mailboxes** — juan@, carlo@, cherry@. Search for `docusign`, `purchase agreement`, `escrow`, `disclosure`. Pull the addresses off those threads.
3. **Any CRM or deal spreadsheet** that tracks transactions.
4. **Ask Cherry directly.** She will remember counterparties no system recorded.

---

## Then

Put them in one column, one address per line. Settings → Blocklist → Import.

It applies to every campaign at once, forever, whatever list an address later turns up on. That is the whole point: it cannot be bypassed by a badly built list, which is what happened here.

**Then add whole domains** where the firm should never be contacted. Instantly's blocklist accepts `@greenbanker.com` as well as individual addresses. One entry covers everyone there.

---

## How you know it worked

Search the blocklist for `stanleylo@greenbanker.com`. It is already there from 22 September. When the rest of the client list is in, run the same check on two or three names Cherry gives you off the top of her head. If they are all in, it is done.
