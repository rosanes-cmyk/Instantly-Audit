    Seven words worth knowing

    These come up on every tab. One plain sentence each.
    Instantly
    The tool that sends our emails and collects the replies. Everything here happens inside it.
    Domain
    The part after the @. We own two: twinhomebuyer.com for real business, and metrixgenerate.com for sales email, so problems stay off the first one.
    Inbox
    One email address we send from. We have nine. Spreading emails across several stops any one of them looking like a spammer.
    Bounce rate
    How many emails never arrive, usually because the address is dead. Under 2% is fine. Over 5% and Gmail starts blocking us.
    Do-not-email list
    People we must never email again. Anyone who asks us to stop goes on it, and it covers every campaign at once.
    Unsubscribe link
    The “stop emailing me” link. The law says every sales email must have one that works.
    Deliverability
    Whether our email lands in someone’s inbox or their spam folder.
